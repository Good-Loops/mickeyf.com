# Brevo inactivity-warning preparation

The owner selected Brevo's free plan as the candidate for automated inactivity warnings while retaining Gmail for parent-form correspondence. A Free account and authenticated `notify.mickeyf.com` sender domain were set up on 4 November 2026. The warning sender is `accounts@notify.mickeyf.com`; parent correspondence remains at the existing support inbox. This adapter is not mounted, scheduled or enabled in production, and no family's information has been sent.

An owner-controlled English synthetic sample reached Gmail's Inbox with SPF, DKIM and DMARC passing, the intended From address and the existing support Reply-To. The sandbox request separately returned acceptance without delivery. Live event reports use numeric UTC offsets and the `requests` event name; empty reports were observed before delivery events became available. Neither an accepted request nor an initially empty report establishes delivery.

**Tracking decision:** the received sample was HTML with open-tracking pixels despite `textContent` and `contactPixelTrackingConsent: false`. Brevo's [21 July 2026 clarification](https://developers.brevo.com/changelog/2026/7/21) says the flag anonymizes opens; it does not remove the pixel. After reviewing that finding, the owner explicitly accepted anonymous tracking on 4 November 2026. Require account-wide transactional tracking anonymization as well as per-contact consent enforcement and explicit false flags; disclose anonymous aggregate tracking accurately rather than promising no pixels. Recipient-bound delivery/bounce events remain necessary for account safety; identifiable engagement events remain a hold. One-month transactional log retention and no stored previews were observed, but provider/regional review, full delivery/failure testing and production readiness remain incomplete.

`brevoInactivityMail.ts` prepares one fixed, version-bound bilingual notice to exactly one recipient. The caller must first persist and authorize a unique attempt bound to the current parent/child relationship, current verified contact and activity snapshot. Brevo's response means only that it accepted the request. A timeout, malformed response, rate limit or any other uncertain result is a hold requiring reconciliation; the adapter does not retry. Brevo's [single-message idempotency documentation](https://developers.brevo.com/changelog/2021/11/10) describes a 15-minute window, while its [batch documentation](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/heterogenous-versions-batch-emails) describes 30 minutes. Neither replaces a durable outbox or makes later retries safe.

The experimental read path queries Brevo's [authenticated event report](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/get-email-event-report) for the exact message ID and matches the exact sender and recipient. It treats missing, mismatched, truncated, unknown or failed events as a hold and rounds imprecise event times forward. An open or click event bound to that recipient/message also holds for investigation because only anonymous engagement tracking is permitted. A reported delivery is only an observation of the recipient server accepting mail; it does not prove inbox placement, reading, or a final forwarded destination. Known Apple relay domains and ambiguous `icloud.com` addresses remain held even on a delivery observation. Query results can arrive late, be unavailable, or be affected by retention; the adapter cannot prove completeness or the absence of a subsequent bounce. No response from the provider is authority to delete.

Before activation, implement and qualify all of the following together:

1. A durable, separately protected send-attempt and event ledger, locking and exact notice/contact/relationship bindings, duplicate handling, continuous reconciliation, and monitoring of later bounces, complaints, provider outages, changed addresses, and database restores. A restore must never move the warning or activity clock backward. The independent child-activity continuity guard must be wired to every relevant writer and qualified.
2. A privacy and regional assessment of the full provider and subprocessor data flow and contract, including onward transfers and the United States and Brazil. Brevo says its database hosting is in the EU, but that alone does not establish where all processing or recipients are located. Update notices and store disclosures to match the actual configuration.
3. Owner review of the applicable account terms and data-processing terms, then a verified sender domain, SPF/DKIM, Apple private-relay sender registration, a narrowly scoped key in protected secret storage, and appropriate quotas. Brevo says it may replace an unauthenticated or free-address sender, so verify the actual From address before relying on an event binding. The warning uses the existing public `mickeyf.plays@gmail.com` support mailbox as Reply-To; confirm real direct and Apple-relay replies reach that mailbox, and confirm both the sending domain and reply mailbox satisfy Apple's relay requirements. Do not place credentials, forms, parent details or child identifiers in code, logs, tests, or URLs. Parent forms continue to use the existing owner-operated Gmail process. [Brevo sender authentication](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/14925263522578-Comply-with-Gmail-Yahoo-and-Microsoft-s-requirements-for-email-senders)
4. Configure and verify provider [log retention and preview settings](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4415743225746-Configure-a-custom-retention-period-for-your-transactional-logs-and-email-previews) before using real recipients: use the shortest qualified retention and do not store previews. Enable [anonymous transactional tracking](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/11643306229906-Can-I-anonymize-the-tracking-of-opens-and-clicks-for-my-emails), keep per-contact consent enforcement enabled, unknown consent set to No, and each API recipient's `contactPixelTrackingConsent` explicitly false. Inspect received source and provider reports: pixels and anonymous aggregate counts are permitted, recipient-linked engagement is not. Do not use opens or clicks as activity, acknowledgement, delivery, or deletion evidence. Independently retain only the minimum evidence necessary for the policy. Define removal of provider-held data when a family is deleted.
5. Account-health monitoring and owner notifications: Brevo says an unused free account may be deleted after four months, while automatic activity can count as use. Do not assume availability years later. Handle its daily sending limit and queue, which can defer or drop messages. Keep engagement tracking anonymous and independent from inactivity/deletion decisions.
6. Synthetic direct and Apple-relay sends with owner-controlled addresses, including failures, duplicate attempts, stale or missing events, forwarding disabled, sender-domain authentication, Spam, outage, rate limits, and restore/replay. First verify recovery of the encrypted form archive on a separate device. Such tests do not substitute for real guardian review.
7. Verify the actual child-facing and parent-facing UI, account/retention notice, both supported regional configurations and a creation-closed rollback. Keep the production inactivity deployment and child-creation guards in place until all prerequisites pass.

The [free plan](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208589409-About-Brevo-s-pricing-plans) currently advertises 300 messages per day. Sending approval, terms, limits, locations and costs may change. At the limit Brevo may queue or drop messages, so a successful request or an absent bounce never establishes delivery. No plan upgrade or paid add-on is assumed.
