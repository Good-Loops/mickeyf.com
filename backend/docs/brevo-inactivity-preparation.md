# Brevo inactivity-warning preparation

The owner selected Brevo's free plan as the candidate for automated inactivity warnings while retaining Gmail for parent-form correspondence. This code is not mounted, scheduled, provisioned or enabled. No Brevo account has been created and no family's information has been sent.

`brevoInactivityMail.ts` prepares one fixed, version-bound bilingual notice to exactly one recipient. The caller must first persist and authorize a unique attempt bound to the current parent/child relationship, current verified contact and activity snapshot. Brevo's response means only that it accepted the request. A timeout, malformed response, rate limit or any other uncertain result is a hold requiring reconciliation; the adapter does not retry. Brevo's [single-message idempotency documentation](https://developers.brevo.com/changelog/2021/11/10) describes a 15-minute window, while its [batch documentation](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/heterogenous-versions-batch-emails) describes 30 minutes. Neither replaces a durable outbox or makes later retries safe.

The experimental read path queries Brevo's [authenticated event report](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/get-email-event-report) for the exact message ID and matches the exact sender and recipient. It treats missing, mismatched, truncated, unknown or failed events as a hold and rounds imprecise event times forward. A reported delivery is only an observation of the recipient server accepting mail; it does not prove inbox placement, reading, or a final forwarded destination. Known Apple relay domains and ambiguous `icloud.com` addresses remain held even on a delivery observation. Query results can arrive late, be unavailable, or be affected by retention; the adapter cannot prove completeness or the absence of a subsequent bounce. No response from the provider is authority to delete.

Before activation, implement and qualify all of the following together:

1. A durable, separately protected send-attempt and event ledger, locking and exact notice/contact/relationship bindings, duplicate handling, continuous reconciliation, and monitoring of later bounces, complaints, provider outages, changed addresses, and database restores. A restore must never move the warning or activity clock backward. The independent child-activity continuity guard must be wired to every relevant writer and qualified.
2. A privacy and regional assessment of the full provider and subprocessor data flow and contract, including onward transfers and the United States and Brazil. Brevo says its database hosting is in the EU, but that alone does not establish where all processing or recipients are located. Update notices and store disclosures to match the actual configuration.
3. Owner review of the applicable account terms and data-processing terms, then a verified sender domain, SPF/DKIM, Apple private-relay sender registration, a narrowly scoped key in protected secret storage, and appropriate quotas. Brevo says it may replace an unauthenticated or free-address sender, so verify the actual From address before relying on an event binding. The warning uses the existing public `mickeyf.plays@gmail.com` support mailbox as Reply-To; confirm real direct and Apple-relay replies reach that mailbox, and confirm both the sending domain and reply mailbox satisfy Apple's relay requirements. Do not place credentials, forms, parent details or child identifiers in code, logs, tests, or URLs. Parent forms continue to use the existing owner-operated Gmail process. [Brevo sender authentication](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/14925263522578-Comply-with-Gmail-Yahoo-and-Microsoft-s-requirements-for-email-senders)
4. Configure and verify provider [log retention and preview settings](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4415743225746-Configure-a-custom-retention-period-for-your-transactional-logs-and-email-previews) before using real recipients: logs are indefinite by default; use the shortest qualified retention and do not store previews. Enable Brevo's [per-contact tracking-consent control](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/37113920427922-About-email-tracking-pixels-and-the-CNIL-recommendation-in-Brevo), set unknown consent to no tracking, and verify that the API's explicit `contactPixelTrackingConsent: false` suppresses both open and click tracking. Brevo reports individual tracking is on by default; the API field must not be assumed effective without its account setting. Independently retain only the minimum evidence necessary for the policy. Define removal of provider-held data when a family is deleted.
5. Account-health monitoring and owner notifications: Brevo says an unused free account may be deleted after four months, while automatic activity can count as use. Do not assume availability years later. Handle its daily sending limit and queue, which can defer or drop messages. Do not turn on tracking pixels or click analytics for this workflow.
6. Synthetic direct and Apple-relay sends with owner-controlled addresses, including failures, duplicate attempts, stale or missing events, forwarding disabled, sender-domain authentication, Spam, outage, rate limits, and restore/replay. First verify recovery of the encrypted form archive on a separate device. Such tests do not substitute for real guardian review.
7. Verify the actual child-facing and parent-facing UI, account/retention notice, both supported regional configurations and a creation-closed rollback. Keep the production inactivity deployment and child-creation guards in place until all prerequisites pass.

The [free plan](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208589409-About-Brevo-s-pricing-plans) currently advertises 300 messages per day. Sending approval, terms, limits, locations and costs may change. At the limit Brevo may queue or drop messages, so a successful request or an absent bounce never establishes delivery. No plan upgrade or paid add-on is assumed.
