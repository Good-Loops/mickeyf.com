# Frontend asset provenance

On 31 October 2026, the project owner confirmed that they created or generated
the p4-Vega animated sprites and the website's space backgrounds, and that they
created the p4-Vega background music. This records the owner's confirmation; it
does not assert that AI-generated material has exclusive copyright protection.

The confirmation covers:

- `ts/assets/sprites/p4Vega/`: `p4.png`, `water.png`, `bhBlue.png`, `bhRed.png`,
  and `bhYellow.png`.
- `ts/assets/backgrounds/`: the galaxy, nebula, quasar, Wolf-Rayet and star
  artwork in the current frontend.
- `ts/assets/audio/bg-sound-p4.mp3`.

The separate native icon/splash sources and their generation history are
documented in `resources/README.md`. Frontend font sources and SIL Open Font
License copies are documented in `public/fonts/README.md`. Three Bosses has its
own `unity/three-bosses/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

When adding a new asset, record its origin and any applicable permission or
license alongside it; this confirmation does not cover assets added later.
