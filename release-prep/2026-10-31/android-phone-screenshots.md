# Android phone screenshots

Captured on 31 October 2026 from the actual `Ludolume Staging` Android app on a
Moto G04s running Android 14. The separately installed debug build used an
unconnected test API address and no real account. Its SHA-256 was
`be68a046859a932eccce6befbf1e489ff8bb831a87c3f37fca8b84f9c28bc4ff`.

The phone was temporarily set to 1080 × 1920 at 420 dpi to capture a 9:16
image at Play's recommended resolution while preserving the original logical
width. Its physical 720 × 1612 display size and 280 dpi were restored afterward.
The captures were converted from opaque RGBA PNG to 24-bit RGB PNG without
altering their visible content. They show actual p4-Vega gameplay and the
Dancing Circles animation, not mockups.

These screenshots are draft listing assets, not evidence that the final
Play-signed bundle, account features, or production backend have been tested.
Recheck them against the actual release before submission, including the
